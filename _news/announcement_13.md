We introduce <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17053">ContextRL</a>, a **context-aware RL** objective that rewards models for **selecting the context that supports an answer**, improving **long-horizon agentic** and **multimodal reasoning** by +2.2% and +1.8% over standard GRPO.
